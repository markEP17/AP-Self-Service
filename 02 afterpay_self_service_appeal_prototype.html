My Afterpay

Customer self-service appeal journey

This prototype mirrors the reference app's mint-and-white visual language while adding a full customer-facing appeal submission and post-submission tracking experience.

1
Start appealExplain the review process, expected turnaround, and what information the customer will need.
2
Capture appeal reasonCustomer selects the closest reason and can add context before proceeding.
3
Upload evidenceSupport ID, passport, driver's license, utility bill, and other documentary evidence.
4
Review and submitPresent a final confirmation screen before creating the case.
5
Ticket confirmationShow the ticket number immediately and confirm that email updates will be sent.
6
Track the appealCustomers can return to My Afterpay and see all appeals plus a detailed progress timeline.
Design language used: soft mint backgrounds, high-contrast black CTAs, rounded white content cards, generous whitespace, compact explanatory copy, and a single primary task per screen. The status tracker uses the same visual system rather than introducing a separate support-center aesthetic.
